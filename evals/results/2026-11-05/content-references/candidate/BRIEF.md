# Relay product notes

Relay is a browser tool for a small service business to collect customer requests, assign responsibility, and see request status. The initial visitor action is to request access. Pricing has not been decided.

Rough copy: Requests arrive through messages and get lost. Give each request an owner. See what is waiting. Keep the conversation together. “Teams resolve requests twice as fast” is a draft claim for which no study or customer evidence has been supplied.

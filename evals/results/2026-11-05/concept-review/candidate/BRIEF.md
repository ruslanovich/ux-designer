# Original task

A customer chooses between three service plans. Before committing, the customer must compare the monthly total, minimum commitment, included service visits, and cancellation cost for each option. All three options and those facts are available from the product catalog.

# Chosen plan

Show only the most popular plan and a Continue button. Reveal its price and terms after Continue, then allow purchase. The other two options appear only after the customer returns to a separate catalog screen. The selected plan and its terms are cleared on return.

# Implemented behavior

The implementation follows the plan exactly. It shows the most popular option, reveals only that option's facts after Continue, and clears them when returning to the catalog. There is no view or retained selection that presents the options together. The supplied task has not changed.

Available evidence is this description; no browser or backend execution is represented by it.
